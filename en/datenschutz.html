Creox GmbH processes personal data in accordance with the Swiss FADP and, where applicable, the EU GDPR.

Contact and demo forms

When you use the forms we process name, company, email, phone, selected topics, your website if given, and your message solely to answer your enquiry. You have the right to access, correct and delete your data.

Analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics (Vercel Inc., USA). No cookies are set and no identifiers are stored in your browser; visitors are distinguished by a hash computed from the incoming request, which is discarded after 24 hours. We record the time of the visit, the page viewed, the referring page, approximate origin (country, region, city), operating system, browser and device type. Analysis is aggregated only; no data is linked to a person or an IP address.

Processors

This website is hosted by Vercel (USA). Form data is stored with Supabase (data centre in the EU); e-mail notifications are sent via Resend (USA). Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (Google, USA), which transmits your IP address to Google. For transfers to the USA we rely on appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses or the Data Privacy Framework).
