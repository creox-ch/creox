Design systems & quality

On-brand, checked, approved.

  • Every brand has its own design system. Builders for posts, mailings and pages are generated from it.
  • Typography rules and an anti-slop check: no generic gradients, no placeholder icons, no orphaned prepositions.
  • Fact-checking with sources and a check date on articles.
  • Only what a person has approved gets published.
