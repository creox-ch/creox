The people

Substance, not show.

A compact core team that actually delivers projects – with experience across events, marketing and technology, and a network of specialists.

Yves Vollenweider

Managing director & co-founder

As managing director of Creox GmbH, Yves brings together 30+ years across the motorcycle, trade-fair and event world – from 12 years as Director Event Management at Messe Zürich and running the SWISS-MOTO to marketing lead at BMW Motorrad Switzerland. His standard: projects that work, not just look good.

yves@creox.ch

Kseniia Chudina

Co-founder · Head of Marketing & ICT

As co-founder and partner of Creox GmbH, Kseniia is our mind for new technologies. Specialised in CRM and web solutions, she builds digital platforms with her team and sets bold new accents. For her, AI isn't a buzzword but a tool – used deliberately, it makes projects faster, smarter and more effective.
