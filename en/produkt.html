Marketing Assistant

One Claude core on your brand data: content, communication and research — with your approval before anything is published.

In use
Problem
SMEs, event organisers and communities lack the people for steady, on-brand content across every channel.
What it does
Claude, connected via MCP to brand data, design system and channels. Agents for content, communication and research prepare the work — a person approves it.
Where it runs
MOTO-ZÜRICH, SVOI, Frankenplatz, Cadre (ex Kunstwerkstube).
Role of Claude
The core of the product: copy, carousels, mailings, articles, classification of community messages, reports.
Status
Running on our brands; first external clients onboarding.

Product

MotoGen

The platform for motorcyclists in Switzerland — and the digital backbone of MOTO-ZÜRICH.

In development
Problem
Motorcyclists in Switzerland have no single place for their bike, routes, clubs, events and services.
What it does
Digital garage, road map, challenges and badges, clubs, group rides, events, a catalogue of services and parts, QR profile. For MOTO-ZÜRICH: exhibitor portal (contract in one click), interactive hall plan, visitor account.
Where it runs
MOTO-ZÜRICH 2027 (19–21 February) — exhibitor portal and hall plan.
Role of Claude
Route and event suggestions, answers to exhibitors on the regulations, exhibitor acquisition emails, dealer leads from the visitor base.
Status
Exhibitor portal for the 2027 edition.

Audio Guide

QR code at the artwork → the story of the work and music written for it. Connected to the art catalogue le-cadre.ch.
