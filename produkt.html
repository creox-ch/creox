Product in focus · MotoGen

Your bike. Your tracks. Your crew.

MotoGen is the platform for motorcyclists in Switzerland. It brings your whole riding life into one place: the machines you own, the roads you've ridden, the people you ride with. Everything below fits together into your personal “genome” — and connects to a QR profile, a points wallet and your event tickets.

The “Genome”

Not a navi, not a marketplace. A living profile that grows with every ride, bike and connection.

Garage
Every bike you own or owned, with specs, mods, service history and photos. Your digital garage that stays with you when you buy or sell.
Map
The roads you've ridden, saved and shared. Discover routes, mark favourites and build a map of where you and your crew have been.
Challenges
Passes, routes and seasonal goals turned into challenges. Collect badges and points as you ride — and see how you stack up.
Clubs
Find and run clubs and communities. Shared feeds, member lists and a home base for every group you ride with.
Ride together
Post a ride or join one. Match with riders nearby, plan a route together and roll out as a group.
Events
Rides, meet-ups, track days and shows — including MOTO-ZÜRICH. Save events, get your QR ticket and keep it in your wallet.
Services
Trusted garages, dealers and specialists near you. Book service, find parts and keep your bike's history in one place.
QR profile & wallet
One QR code for your rider profile, plus a wallet that holds your points and your event tickets — ready to scan at MOTO-ZÜRICH.
